Everything you need to stay on track

Six simple tools, built for students with a full schedule.

Weekly planner

See every subject across the week on one screen and move sessions with a tap.

Deadline countdown

Each assignment shows how many days are left, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Focus timer

Start a 25-minute study block, then take a short break. Repeat when ready.

Subject colours

Give each subject its own colour to spot your balance at a glance.

Progress tracking

Tick off tasks and watch your daily progress bar fill up.

Works on any device

Plan on your laptop, check your list on your phone between classes.

How it works

  1. Add your subjects

    List your courses and pick a colour for each one.

  2. Enter your deadlines

    Add exam and assignment dates. FocusFlow spreads the work across your week.

  3. Follow today's checklist

    Open the app each day, finish the tasks, and track your progress.
